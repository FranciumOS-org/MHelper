MHelper — G-Helper for macOS on ASUS laptops
==============================================

1. THE KEXT (once, with OpenCore)

   Automatic: open Terminal, type  sudo  and a space, drag Extras/install.sh
   into the window, press Return and enter your password. It finds your
   OpenCore EFI, copies MHelper.kext into EFI/OC/Kexts, adds it to config.plist
   (keeping a backup, config.plist.pre-mhelper) and tells you to restart.
   Undo with Extras/uninstall.sh the same way.

   By hand:
   - Mount your EFI partition.
   - Copy MHelper.kext into  EFI/OC/Kexts/
   - Add it to config.plist (Kernel > Add). Easiest: open config.plist in
     ProperTree and press Cmd+Shift+R (OC Snapshot). Or add an entry by hand:
         Arch            x86_64
         BundlePath      MHelper.kext
         Enabled         True
         ExecutablePath  Contents/MacOS/MHelper
         PlistPath       Contents/Info.plist
         MinKernel       22.0.0
   - Reboot.

   No Lilu needed. MHelper works next to AsusSMC.

2. THE APP

   - Drag MHelper.app to Applications and open it.
   - First time only, macOS will say it can't check the app (it isn't
     notarised). Right-click MHelper.app > Open > Open.
     Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MHelper.app
   - MHelper lives in the menu bar (top right), not in the Dock.
   - In its panel, tick "Open at login".

That's it. The app only shows what your laptop supports.

GOOD TO KNOW

   - Loading MHelper changes nothing; every change comes from you.
   - Keyboard colours reset at reboot unless you click "Save to firmware".
   - Eco mode turns the NVIDIA GPU off, also in Windows, until you turn Eco
     off again (a reboot may be needed for it to come back).

EXTRAS FOLDER

   - mhelper            command-line version (run ./mhelper for help)
   - rogmouse           ROG mouse settings from Terminal (run ./rogmouse)
   - MHelperProbe.kext  lists what your laptop's firmware supports. Please
                        send its output with your model to the GitHub issues:
                            sudo Extras/load.sh probe
   - install.sh         put the kext in your EFI (see step 1);
     uninstall.sh       uninstall.sh switches it off again
   - load.sh/unload.sh  try the kext without putting it in your EFI
                        (needs unsigned kexts allowed; gone after a reboot):
                            sudo Extras/load.sh
